# Bureau of Indian Standards Act 2016 — Statutory Framework

## Section 13 — Grant and Renewal of License
Under Section 13 of the BIS Act 2016, the Bureau of Indian Standards is the sole statutory authority empowered to grant, renew, suspend, or cancel a license to use the Standard Mark (ISI mark) or grant a Certificate of Conformity / Compulsory Registration. Third-party consulting platforms and software tools (such as ISense) are independent technical assistance providers and have no legal authority to issue licenses, certify products, or approve regulatory submissions.

## Section 16 — Mandatory Quality Control Orders
The Central Government, by order published in the Official Gazette, may direct that any goods, article, process, system or service of any scheduled industry shall conform to an Indian Standard and direct the use of the Standard Mark under a license or certificate of conformity. No person shall manufacture, import, distribute, sell, or expose for sale any goods covered under a mandatory Quality Control Order (QCO) without a valid license or registration issued by BIS.

## Section 29 — Penalties for Unauthorized Use
Any person who makes unauthorized use of the Standard Mark or contravenes mandatory notification orders under Section 16 shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term up to two years or with fine which shall not be less than two lakh rupees, extending up to ten times the value of goods produced or sold.
