# IS 4151 : 2020 — Protective Helmets for Two-Wheeler Riders (Specification)

## Clause 1 — Scope
This standard covers the requirements regarding material, construction, workmanship, finish, and performance tests for protective helmets for everyday two-wheeler riders.

## Clause 7 — Visor Optical Performance and Clarity
Visors must deliver not less than 85% luminous transmittance for clear visors. Visors shall produce no optical distortion, double image, or astigmatism exceeding 0.12 diopters. Scratch resistance and mechanical impact shatter resistance must be verified without cracking into sharp fragments.

## Clause 9.1 — Impact Deceleration Attenuation Test
Helmets undergo conditioning under:
1. Ambient conditioning (+25°C).
2. High temperature conditioning (+50°C).
3. Low temperature conditioning (-10°C).
4. Continuous water immersion.
Helmets are dropped onto flat steel and hemispherical steel anvils at an impact velocity of 7.5 m/s. The peak acceleration measured by a calibrated triaxial accelerometer fitted within the headform shall not exceed 300g (3000 m/s²), and the duration of acceleration exceeding 150g shall not exceed 5.0 milliseconds.

## Clause 9.2 — Retention System (Chinstrap) Dynamic Strength
The retention system is tested on a retention test rig with an initial preload of 50N, followed by a dynamic drop impulse mass of 10.0 kg falling through a height of 750 mm. The maximum dynamic extension shall not exceed 25 mm, and residual permanent elongation shall not exceed 15 mm. The quick-release buckle must open under a release force between 10N and 30N after dynamic loading.
