# IS 9873 (Part 1) : 2019 — Safety of Toys (Part 1: Mechanical and Physical Properties)

## Clause 1 — Scope
Applies to all toys designed or intended for use in play by children under 14 years of age. Under DPIIT Toys Quality Control Order, all imported and domestic toys must carry the ISI Mark.

## Clause 4.4 — Small Parts Ingestion and Choking Hazard
Toys intended for children under 36 months of age, and components separable during normal play or abuse testing (drop test, torque test, tension test), shall not fit entirely within the standard small parts test cylinder (inner diameter 31.7 mm, depth 25.4 mm to 57.1 mm).

## Clause 4.8 — Sharp Edges and Points
Accessible metal and glass edges shall be folded, rolled, or covered with protective plastic caps. Points are tested on the standardized sharp point tester: an indicator lamp illuminates if a point penetrates 0.50 mm under a contact force of 4.5 N. Points that penetrate fail compliance.

## Clause 5.24 — Drop and Mechanical Abuse Testing
Toys undergo 5 consecutive drops from a height of 850 ± 50 mm onto a 4 mm thick steel plate mounted over concrete. Separated fragments must not exhibit sharp edges, sharp points, or small choking parts.
