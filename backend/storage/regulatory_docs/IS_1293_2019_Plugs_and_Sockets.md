# IS 1293 : 2019 — Plugs and Socket-Outlets up to 250V and 16A

## Clause 1 — Scope
Applies to plugs and fixed or portable socket-outlets for AC only, with or without earthing contact, rated between 2.5A and 16A at voltages up to 250V, including smart plugs and Wi-Fi sockets.

## Clause 9 — Dimensional Verification and Gauges
Plug pins shall have solid cylindrical construction (solid brass or nickel plated). Shuttered socket-outlets are mandatory for all domestic installations to prevent contact of children with live apertures. Gauge testing verifies insertion depth, pin diameter, and centre-to-centre pin spacing conforming to Sheet 1 (6A) and Sheet 2 (16A).

## Clause 13.3 — Glow-Wire Thermal Resistance Test
Insulating components retaining current-carrying parts in position are subjected to a glow-wire loop heated to 750°C (or 850°C for unattended smart plugs) applied for 30 seconds. Flames or glowing must extinguish within 30 seconds of removing the glow-wire tip, and burning droplets must not ignite tissue paper placed below.

## Clause 19 — Electric Strength and Insulation Resistance
Insulation resistance between live poles and accessible metal parts must be not less than 5.0 Megohms at 500V DC. Dielectric electric strength withstand of 2000V AC rms applied for 60 seconds without flashover.
