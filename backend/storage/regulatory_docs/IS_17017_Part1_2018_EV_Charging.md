# IS 17017 (Part 1) : 2018 — Electric Vehicle Conductive Charging System (Part 1: General Requirements)

## Clause 1 — Scope
Covers dedicated off-board equipment and AC/DC conductive electric vehicle supply equipment (EVSE) for charging electric road vehicles, with AC supply voltages up to 1000V and DC output voltages up to 1500V.

## Clause 6.4 — Control Pilot Signal & Functional Interlock
The EVSE must communicate with the vehicle using a pulse-width modulated (PWM) pilot signal on the control pilot line. Power energization to the charging cable connector is prohibited until handshake confirmation establishes that the vehicle connector is fully mated, earth continuity is verified, and the vehicle requests ventilation/charge mode.

## Clause 7 — Ingress Protection and Mechanical Durability
Outdoor charging stations and wallboxes must provide minimum ingress protection of IP54 in mated and unmated states. Vehicle connectors must withstand a drive-over mechanical crush test of 5000 N applied via automobile tire without cracking or loss of dielectric isolation.
