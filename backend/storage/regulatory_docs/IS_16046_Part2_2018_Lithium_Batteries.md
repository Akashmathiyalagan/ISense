# IS 16046 (Part 2) : 2018 — Secondary Cells and Batteries (Lithium Systems)

## Clause 1 — Scope
Specifies requirements and tests for the safe operation of portable sealed secondary lithium cells and batteries (including power banks, mobile phone batteries, laptop batteries) containing non-acid electrolytes under intended use and reasonably foreseeable misuse.

## Clause 7.3.2 — External Short Circuit Test
Fully charged battery packs and cells shall be short-circuited by connecting the positive and negative terminals with an external resistance of 80 ± 20 mOhms at 55 ± 5°C. The short circuit condition is maintained until the case temperature drops back below peak by 20%. The cell or battery pack shall not explode, catch fire, or exceed 150°C during test duration.

## Clause 7.3.3 — Free Fall / Drop Test
Each cell or battery is dropped three times from a height of 1.0 meter onto a smooth, horizontal concrete surface in each of the three mutually perpendicular axes. The test specimens shall exhibit no fire, explosion, or chemical electrolyte leakage.

## Clause 7.3.5 — Thermal Abuse Test
The cell is placed in a gravity convection or circulating air oven heated at a rate of 5°C/min to 130 ± 2°C and held at this temperature for 10 minutes. Cells shall not catch fire or explode.

## Clause 7.3.6 — Overcharging Test for Battery Packs
The battery pack is charged from discharged state with a charging current of 2.0 times rated capacity (2C) up to maximum allowable voltage limits. Protection circuits must disconnect charging before catastrophic failure or thermal runaway occurs.
