# IS 16102 (Part 1) : 2012 — Self-Ballasted LED-Lamps for General Lighting Services (Part 1: Safety Requirements)

## Clause 1 — Scope
This standard specifies the safety and interchangeability requirements, together with the test methods and conditions required to show compliance of LED lamps with integrated means for controlling, intended for domestic and similar general lighting purposes. Applies to self-ballasted LED lamps having rated wattage up to 60W and rated voltage up to 250V AC.

## Clause 4.1 — General Safety Design
Self-ballasted LED lamps shall be so designed and constructed that in normal use they function reliably and cause no danger to persons or surroundings. Compliance is checked by carrying out all the tests specified.

## Clause 5 — Marking Protocol
Lamps shall be clearly and durably marked with:
1. Mark of origin (manufacturer name or registered trademark).
2. Rated supply voltage or voltage range (Volts).
3. Rated wattage (Watts).
4. Rated frequency (Hertz).
5. Rated lamp current or luminous flux.
Marking legibility is verified by rubbing for 15 seconds with a cloth soaked in water, and for 15 seconds with a cloth soaked in petroleum spirit. After the test, the marking must remain legible.

## Clause 8.0 — Insulation Resistance and Electric Strength
Insulation resistance between live parts and accessible metal parts shall not be less than 4.0 Megohms when tested at 500V DC applied for 1 minute under ambient damp heat conditioning.
Electric strength test: High-voltage insulation withstand of 1500V AC rms (or 2120V DC) applied between live parts and accessible parts for 1 minute without dielectric breakdown or flashover.

## Clause 9 — Mechanical Strength (Cap Torque Resistance)
The lamp cap shall remain firmly attached to the lamp body when subjected to a minimum mechanical torque:
- B22d and E27 caps: minimum 3.0 Newton-meters (Nm) retention torque.
- E14 caps: minimum 1.15 Newton-meters (Nm) retention torque.
Testing is carried out using a standardized cap torque test fixture without loosening or electrical disruption.

## Clause 12 — Creepage Distance and Clearance
Clearance and creepage distances between live parts and accessible metallic surfaces shall conform to Table 1: minimum creepage distance is 3.0mm, and minimum clearance distance is 2.5mm for rated voltage 250V.
