# BIS Product Certification Scheme (Scheme I — ISI Mark) — Guidelines & Document Checklist

## Clause 1 — Applicability and Certification Route
Scheme I (Product Certification Scheme) applies to goods covered under mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCO) and voluntary ISI certification, including protective helmets (IS 4151), electric ceiling fans (IS 374), and toys (IS 9873). Certification under Scheme I requires demonstrated in-house manufacturing capability, internal testing facilities conforming to the Scheme of Testing and Inspection (STI), and factory audit by BIS inspecting officers.

## Clause 2 — Mandatory Document Checklist for Domestic Manufacturers
For domestic manufacturers applying via the BIS Manak Online portal, the following documents are mandatory:
1. Proof of Manufacturing Premises: Factory registration certificate, municipal trade license, or Consent to Operate (CTO) from the State Pollution Control Board.
2. Machinery & Infrastructure: Detailed list of in-house manufacturing machinery, plant layout diagram, and process manufacturing flowchart.
3. In-House Testing Laboratory: List of testing equipment installed as per the relevant Scheme of Testing and Inspection (STI) along with valid calibration certificates issued by an accredited laboratory.
4. Competent Technical Staff: Appointment letters, educational certificates, and experience records of quality control testing personnel.
5. Brand & Trademark Documents: Registered Trademark certificate or trademark application acknowledgement. If manufacturing for another brand owner, an official Trademark Authorization / Agreement is mandatory.
6. Raw Material Control: Bill of materials (BOM), raw material test certificates, and test purchase receipts.

## Clause 3 — Conditional Requirements for Foreign Manufacturers (FMCS)
When the manufacturing facility is located outside India (Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme - FMCS):
1. Authorized Indian Representative (AIR): An Indian citizen or legal entity residing in India must be formally nominated as the AIR via an executed agreement and Power of Attorney.
2. In-Country Testing: Verification samples drawn during the overseas factory inspection must be sealed and dispatched to a designated BIS-recognized laboratory in India for testing.
3. Commercial & Visa Remittances: Foreign remittance approval and travel indemnity for the BIS inspection team.

## Clause 4 — Step-by-Step Application Procedure
1. Step 1 (Portal Registration): Register the manufacturing unit on the official BIS Manak Online portal (www.manakonline.in).
2. Step 2 (Dossier Filing): Submit Form-V with all factory, machinery, quality control, and testing facility documents.
3. Step 3 (Statutory Fee): Pay application filing fee (₹1,000) and factory audit inspection fee.
4. Step 4 (Preliminary Factory Audit): BIS technical inspecting officers carry out on-site inspection of manufacturing and testing infrastructure.
5. Step 5 (Sample Testing): Test samples are drawn during inspection for testing at the factory lab and independent BIS lab.
6. Step 6 (Scrutiny and Grant): Upon satisfactory audit report and passing test reports, BIS issues the Certificate of License (CM/L number).
