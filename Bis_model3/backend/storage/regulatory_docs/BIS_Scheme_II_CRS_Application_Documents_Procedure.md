# BIS Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS — Scheme II) — Guidelines & Document Checklist

## Clause 1 — Applicability and Certification Route
Scheme II (Compulsory Registration Scheme - CRS) applies to electronic and IT products notified under the Compulsory Registration Orders issued by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and DPIIT. Notified products include self-ballasted LED lamps (IS 16102 Part 1), lithium-ion batteries and power banks (IS 16046 Part 2), IT equipment and power adapters (IS 13252 Part 1), and solar inverters (IS 16221 Part 2). Unlike Scheme I, CRS does not require a prior physical factory audit; registration is granted based on passing test reports issued by a BIS-recognized accredited testing laboratory in India.

## Clause 2 — Mandatory Document Checklist for Domestic Manufacturers
For domestic manufacturers applying under CRS via the official BIS CRS portal, the following documents are mandatory:
1. Valid Laboratory Test Report: Complete safety and compliance test report issued by a BIS-recognized / accredited testing laboratory (NABL/LRS). The test report must be issued within 90 days prior to online submission.
2. Form-1 Application: Form-1 generated through the online portal signed by the authorized signatory.
3. Factory Proof / Manufacturing Premise: Business registration document, GST certificate, or Factory License establishing actual physical manufacturing operations.
4. Trademark & Brand Ownership: Trademark registration certificate issued by the Trade Marks Registry. If the applicant is a manufacturing partner (OEM/ODM), an official Brand Owner Authorization Form is mandatory.
5. Statutory Undertaking & Affidavit: Undertaking confirming that products manufactured will strictly comply with the applicable Indian Standard and declaring model series conformity.
6. Authorized Signatory Identification: KYC documents (PAN, Aadhaar, or Passport) and board resolution or authorization letter for the person signing the application.

## Clause 3 — Conditional Requirements for Foreign Manufacturers
When the manufacturing location is outside India:
1. Authorized Indian Representative (AIR): A resident Indian entity or branch office must be formally appointed as the AIR via nomination agreement and affidavit. The AIR represents the foreign manufacturer in all regulatory proceedings.
2. Business Registration of Foreign Factory: Company incorporation certificate or business license from the country of origin, along with apostilled or authenticated translation in English.
3. Brand Authorization for AIR: Letter from brand owner authorizing the AIR to sign declarations and portal submissions.

## Clause 4 — Step-by-Step Application Procedure
1. Step 1 (Laboratory Testing): Submit representative product samples to a BIS-recognized Indian laboratory for testing under the applicable standard (e.g., IS 16102 Part 1 for LED lamps). Obtain passing test report.
2. Step 2 (Portal Profile Creation): Register the manufacturing premises on the official BIS CRS portal (www.crsbis.in).
3. Step 3 (Dossier & Report Upload): Submit Form-1, attach the test report (within 90 days of issuance), and upload factory, trademark, and undertaking documents.
4. Step 4 (Payment of Statutory Fees): Pay the ₹1,000 application fee and ₹50,000 processing fee online.
5. Step 5 (Scrutiny by BIS Officers): Technical scrutiny of test reports, series formulation, and documents by the BIS CRS division.
6. Step 6 (Grant of Registration): Upon successful verification, BIS issues the official Registration Number (R-number) authorizing the CRS standard mark.
