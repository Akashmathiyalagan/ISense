# BIS Financial Regulations 2024 — Fees and Processing Timelines

## Schedule 1.1 — Compulsory Registration Scheme (CRS / Scheme II) Statutory Fees
1. Application Filing Fee: ₹1,000 per application (non-refundable).
2. Processing Fee: ₹50,000 for primary product category (valid for 2 years registration). Additional series models attract ₹10,000 to ₹20,000 scrutiny fee per addition.
3. Laboratory Testing Charges: Incurred separately, payable directly to the recognized testing laboratory (LRS/NABL) depending on testing scope.
4. Renewal Fee: ₹50,000 for two-year renewal extension.

## Schedule 1.2 — Product Certification Scheme (Scheme I / ISI Mark Domestic)
1. Application Fee: ₹1,000 (non-refundable).
2. Factory Inspection Fee: ₹7,000 per man-day for inspecting officers plus actual travel and accommodation expenses.
3. Annual License Fee: ₹1,000 payable upon grant and each year of license tenure.
4. Marking Fee: Calculated based on production unit volume as defined in the Scheme of Testing and Inspection (STI), subject to a minimum statutory marking fee per annum.

## Schedule 2 — Processing Timelines and Service Standards
- CRS (Scheme II): 15 to 30 working days from date of submission of complete technical dossier with valid accredited test report issued within 90 days.
- Scheme I (Domestic ISI Mark): 60 to 90 working days, including preliminary inspection, sample verification, and counter-sample testing.
- Foreign Manufacturers Certification Scheme (FMCS): 90 to 180 working days due to international travel, visa approvals, and customs clearance of verification samples.
