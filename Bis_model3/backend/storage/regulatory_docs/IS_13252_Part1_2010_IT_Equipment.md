# IS 13252 (Part 1) : 2010 — Information Technology Equipment — Safety (Part 1: General Requirements)

## Clause 1 — Scope
Applies to mains-powered or battery-powered information technology equipment, including power adapters, chargers, laptops, mobile phones, printers, and desktop displays.

## Clause 2.1 — Protection Against Electric Shock
Live parts carrying voltages exceeding 42.4V peak AC or 60V DC must be isolated by double insulation or reinforced insulation so that accessible conductive parts remain safe to touch under single fault conditions.

## Clause 2.9 — Creepage Distances and Clearances
Minimum clearance across isolation barriers for reinforced insulation is 4.0 mm, and minimum creepage distance across printed circuit board traces is 5.0 mm for Pollution Degree 2, Material Group III.

## Clause 5.2 — Dielectric Strength (Hi-Pot Test)
Reinforced insulation between primary AC input circuits and accessible secondary DC circuits must withstand a high-voltage test potential of 3000V AC rms (or 4242V DC peak) for 60 seconds without breakdown or excessive leakage current.

## Clause 4.7 — Flammability of Fire Enclosures
External plastic enclosures housing primary components must have a minimum UL94 V-1 flammability rating; for power adapters and unattended chargers, a UL94 V-0 flammability rating is required.
