# IS 16169 : 2014 — Test Procedure of Islanding Prevention Measures for Utility-Interconnected PV Inverters

## Clause 1 — Scope
This standard provides test procedures to evaluate the performance of islanding prevention measures used in utility-interconnected photovoltaic inverters.

## Clause 4.2 — Resonant Load Test Conditions (RLC Tuning)
The test circuit shall couple the inverter under test to an AC power source and an adjustable resonant RLC load having a quality factor (Qf) of 1.0. Active power and reactive power must be balanced to zero flow from the utility grid prior to switch opening.

## Clause 4.3 — Islanding Trip Disconnect Time Threshold
The islanding condition is initiated by opening the utility isolation switch. The disconnect trip time (run-on time) shall not exceed 2.0 seconds under test power levels of 25%, 50%, 100%, and 125% of rated inverter output. Inverters exceeding 2.0 seconds disconnect time fail compliance.
