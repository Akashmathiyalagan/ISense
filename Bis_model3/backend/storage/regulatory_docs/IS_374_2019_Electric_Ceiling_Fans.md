# IS 374 : 2019 — Electric Ceiling Type Fans and Regulators

## Clause 1 — Scope
Applies to ceiling fans with AC induction motors or Brushless DC (BLDC) motors, suitable for domestic and commercial installation, with sweeps between 600 mm and 1500 mm.

## Clause 11 — Mechanical Suspension and Shackle Safety
The suspension shackle, down-rod, and safety pin assembly shall possess adequate mechanical strength. The suspension assembly must withstand an axial tensile load of not less than 1000 N applied for 1 minute without permanent deformation or shear. A secondary safety retention wire or safety split-pin is mandatory.

## Clause 14 — Air Delivery and Service Value
Air delivery is tested in an airtight test chamber with anemometer array:
- For 1200 mm sweep fans: Minimum air delivery is 210 cubic meters per minute (m³/min).
- Minimum Service Value (air delivery divided by input wattage) for BLDC energy-efficient ceiling fans must be not less than 5.0 m³/min/Watt to qualify under Star Rating benchmarks.

## Clause 15 — Temperature Rise and Insulation Endurance
Motor winding temperature rise shall not exceed the permissible limit for the insulation class: Class A (70°C), Class E (85°C), Class B (95°C), measured by the resistance change method after continuous operation until thermal equilibrium.
