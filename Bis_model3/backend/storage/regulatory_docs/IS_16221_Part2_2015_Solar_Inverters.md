# IS 16221 (Part 2) : 2015 — Safety of Power Converters for Photovoltaic Power Systems (Part 2: Inverters)

## Clause 1 — Scope and Application
This Indian Standard covers the particular safety requirements for grid-connected and standalone power converters (inverters) used in photovoltaic (PV) power systems. Applies to utility-interconnected inverters with DC input voltages up to 1500V DC and AC output up to 1000V AC.

## Clause 4 — Protection Against Electric Shock and Isolation
The inverter must maintain complete galvanic or functional isolation between hazardous DC array voltages and touchable enclosure parts. DC insulation resistance must exceed 1.0 Megohm measured at 1000V DC between the array input conductors (shorted together) and protective earth ground.

## Clause 5.1 — Automated Anti-Islanding Protection
Grid-connected photovoltaic inverters shall incorporate an automatic anti-islanding detection system. Upon disconnection from the utility grid or upon line voltage/frequency drifting outside statutory limits, the inverter shall disconnect and cease to energize the utility line within a maximum time of 2.0 seconds.

## Clause 6.3 — Residual Current and Ground Fault Detection
Inverters shall incorporate continuous residual current monitoring (RCMU). If an excessive residual fault current exceeding 300 mA (or 30 mA continuous touch leakage) is detected on the DC or AC circuit, the inverter must trip and isolate within 300 milliseconds.

## Clause 8 — Temperature Limits and Fire Resistance
Enclosure materials must satisfy glow-wire and UL94 flammability requirements. All power semiconductor heat sinks and transformers must operate within specified thermal class limits under 50°C ambient rated temperature.
